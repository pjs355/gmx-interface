Kalshi traded $23.8B in notional volume in 2025, up 1,108% year over year, with 97M transactions and roughly $225M in open interest at year end. Kalshi is a CFTC-regulated U.S. exchange where traders buy and sell event contracts on economics, politics, weather, sports, and other defined outcomes. It is the primary regulated prediction market venue for U.S. traders who want USD settlement under federal oversight.

## Where is Kalshi available?

Kalshi is a **CFTC-regulated U.S. exchange**. Kalshi states its prediction markets are available to users in **all 50 U.S. states, Washington D.C., and U.S. territories** (including Guam and American Samoa).

Kalshi's member agreement prohibits access if you are domiciled in, organized in, or located in any **Restricted Jurisdiction**, including:

Afghanistan, Algeria, Angola, Australia, Belarus, Belgium, Bolivia, Bulgaria, Burkina Faso, Cameroon, Canada, Central African Republic, Côte d'Ivoire, Cuba, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Ethiopia, France, Haiti, Hungary, Iran, Iraq, Italy, Kenya, Laos, Lebanon, Libya, Mali, Monaco, Mozambique, Myanmar (Burma), Namibia, New Zealand, Nicaragua, Niger, North Korea, People's Republic of China, Poland, Russia, Singapore, Somalia, South Sudan, Sudan, Switzerland, Syria, Taiwan, Thailand, Ukraine, United Arab Emirates, United Kingdom, Venezuela, Yemen, Zimbabwe, and any jurisdiction subject to comprehensive U.S. economic sanctions.

Kalshi also restricts **Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs)**, including Members of Congress, presidents, governors, federal judges, and senior military personnel, from trading on some or all markets.

## How does Kalshi trading work?

Kalshi lists event contracts with explicit rulebooks for each market. You buy Yes or No contracts, and prices move through an order book as traders interact. Settlement follows the market's published criteria when the event resolves.

Kalshi uses USD funding through its standard deposit and withdrawal rails. Account verification is required for trading.

Each contract specifies exactly what counts as Yes and what data source resolves the market. Read the rulebook before you size a position. Two markets on the same headline can resolve differently from Polymarket or Limitless contracts.

## What can you trade on Kalshi?

Kalshi covers macro indicators (CPI, Fed decisions), elections, sports, and esports event contracts. Not every event available on Polymarket appears on Kalshi, and vice versa. Contract definitions can differ even when headlines look similar.

Sports dominates Kalshi volume at roughly 80% since mid-2024. That makes Kalshi a primary book for regulated U.S. sports and esports event contracts.

## How do Kalshi fees work?

Kalshi charges takers only on market orders. Resting limit orders that add liquidity (makers) pay **0%**.

Taker fee formula:

**fee = round_up(0.07 × C × P × (1 − P))** to the next cent

Where C is contracts, P is price (0 to 1), and round_up rounds to the nearest cent.

| Role | Fee |
| --- | --- |
| Taker (market order) | round_up(0.07 × C × P × (1 − P)) |
| Maker (resting limit) | 0% |

Fee math example: buy 100 Yes contracts at 50¢ as a taker.

- C = 100, P = 0.50
- Fee = round_up(0.07 × 100 × 0.50 × 0.50) = round_up(1.75) = **$1.75**
- You pay $50.00 + $1.75 = **$51.75** all-in
- If Yes settles at $1, you receive $100. Net profit is **$48.25**

At 50¢ the taker fee peaks at $1.75 per 100 contracts and falls symmetrically toward the extremes.

## How does Kalshi compare to Polymarket?

Polymarket is crypto-native with USDC on Polygon and estimated $21–22B in 2025 notional volume. Kalshi is regulated with USD and skews sports harder. Combined, the two venues account for roughly 97.5% of sector activity.

Cross-venue gaps of 2–5 cents on the same matched event are common when both books list the contract.

## How does Kalshi compare to on-chain sports books?

Kalshi is CFTC-regulated with $23.8B in 2025 notional volume. SX.bet reports roughly $57.8M in 30-day DEX volume on DefiLlama with sports-only focus on SX Rollup. BetDEX runs on Solana via Monaco with Anjouan and Irish Revenue licensing. Kalshi offers regulated USD event contracts; on-chain sports exchanges target crypto-native settlement and different product mixes.

## Who uses Kalshi?

Kalshi fits U.S. traders who want CFTC-regulated USD contracts, deep sports liquidity, and clear rulebooks on macro and weather events. Aggregators like ClutchComet route to Kalshi via DFlow on matched events alongside Polymarket, Limitless, and Predict when Kalshi offers the best integrated price.
