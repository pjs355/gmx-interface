## Why esports needs an aggregator

Esports prices move fast. A pistol round swing, a veto announcement, or a roster tweet can reprice a series contract in seconds. Each venue updates on its own schedule with its own trader pool.

Without an esports prediction market aggregator you are choosing between:

- **Speed:** act on the first price you see
- **Price:** check four apps and miss the window

ClutchComet removes that tradeoff on matched rows. All Odds columns update from live feeds. Smart routing executes on integrated venues in milliseconds, not after 90 seconds of tab switching.

## Esports aggregator workflow on ClutchComet

**Pre-match:** Open the matched row. Compare asks across tradeable columns. Flag gaps larger than **3 cents**.

**On veto:** Re-check within two minutes. Cross-venue gaps open most often here.

**Live:** Watch the stream on ClutchComet. After each map, compare venues immediately. Slower books often lag **1-3 minutes**.

**Execute:** Route manually or use smart order routing. Size down when total book depth is under **3×** your intended position.
