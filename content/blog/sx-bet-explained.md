## Where is SX.bet available?

SX Bet's official blog states the platform **is not available in the United States**. The blog positions SX Bet for a global, non-U.S. audience and settles in USDC.

SX Bet does not publish a full country blocklist in its developer docs. Sign-up supports email, Google, or wallet connection.

## How does trading work on SX.bet?

SX.bet lists sports markets as on-chain exchange contracts on SX Rollup. Traders back outcomes through the platform's order book and parlay products.

Settlement follows SX.bet market rules. Sports-only focus means politics and macro contracts do not appear on SX.

You need an SX.bet account and supported wallet for direct trading. Parlay and exchange features are available on SX directly.

## What markets does SX.bet list?

SX.bet focuses on sports wagering across major leagues and international sports. SX blog positioning compares SX sports depth to Polymarket sports markets. Cross-venue gaps on the same match are common when multiple books list the event.

## How do SX.bet fees work?

SX.bet charges **0% trading fees on single bets** for both makers and takers. Fees apply only to winning parlays.

| Bet type | Maker | Taker | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Singles | 0% | 0% | No fee on entry or exit |
| Parlays | 0% | 5% on profit | Only winning parlays pay; losers pay $0 |

**Single bet fee math example:** buy 100 Yes shares at 50¢.

- Entry = 100 × $0.50 = **$50.00**
- Trading fee = **$0.00**
- If Yes wins: payout = **$100.00**. Net profit = **$50.00**

**Parlay fee math example:** $50 stake on a winning parlay with $50 gross profit.

- Parlay fee = $50.00 profit × 5% = **$2.50**
- Net profit = $50.00 − $2.50 = **$47.50**

SX Bet covers gas fees on betting transactions. Bridging deposits and withdrawals may carry third-party fees from Glide, MoonPay, or PayTrie.

## Why do DefiLlama and SX.bet volume numbers differ?

DefiLlama measures on-chain DEX volume (~$668.6M cumulative). SX.bet's blog cites about $1.2B cumulative sports volume with a broader methodology. Label the metric type when you cite SX stats.

## How does SX.bet compare to Kalshi on sports?

Kalshi traded $23.8B in notional volume in 2025 with sports at roughly 80% of activity since mid-2024. SX.bet reports roughly $57.8M in 30-day DEX volume on DefiLlama. Kalshi offers CFTC-regulated USD event contracts. SX.bet offers sports-native on-chain exchange products on SX Rollup with parlay features.
