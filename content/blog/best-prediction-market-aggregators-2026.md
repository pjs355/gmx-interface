**What it aggregates:** Nine All Odds venues on **matched events** (2,287 rows as of June 2026). Four tradeable: Polymarket, Kalshi, Limitless, Predict. Five comparison-only: Myriad, BetDEX, Forkast, SX.bet, Hyperliquid.

**Best for:** Active traders, especially esports, who want compare-and-execute without four separate accounts.

## Data and research aggregators

**Delphi Terminal** normalizes L1-L2-L3 order book data across multiple exchanges with API access. Strong for quants and funds. No consumer execution layer.

**Your Prediction Edge** compares Kalshi, Polymarket, PredictIt, Metaculus, and Manifold in one dashboard with arbitrage highlighting. Free tier for comparison. No routed trading.

**Full Prediction** and **Prediction Express** focus on consensus probability views across major platforms. Useful for macro and politics research. Not built for esports live trading workflow.

**Meridian Edge** publishes weighted consensus from regulated sources on an API cadence. Sports-focused expansion on roadmap per public materials.

## How to pick a prediction market aggregator

**Choose a data aggregator if** you build models, write research, or spot arb without needing one-click execution.

**Choose ClutchComet if** you trade regularly, line shop across venues, and want one balance with smart routing on matched events.

**Use both if** you research on a data terminal and execute on ClutchComet when you find a trade. Many serious traders use a research layer plus an execution layer.

## What to look for in any aggregator

**Matched event quality.** Do columns represent the same contract, or just similar headlines?

**Fee transparency.** Same ask, different all-in cost after venue fees.

**Execution honesty.** Does the tool hide better prices on venues it does not integrate?

**Catalog scope.** Matched rows vs full venue listings. ClutchComet is explicit: **2,287** matched rows, not global catalogs.

**Live update speed.** Matters most for esports and in-play sports.
