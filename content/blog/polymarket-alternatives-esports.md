Polymarket US is a separate product with its own rollout scope. None of that makes Polymarket bad. It means one venue rarely gives you the best price on every match.

## Kalshi: the regulated alternative

The most direct Polymarket alternative for U.S. traders who want USD and no wallet setup.

Kalshi reported **$23.8B** in notional volume in 2025. It is CFTC-regulated, fiat-native, and available in all 50 U.S. states, Washington D.C., and U.S. territories. Covers CS2 and major esports alongside politics, economics, weather, and sports.

**What it does better than Polymarket:** Easiest onboarding for non-crypto users. Bank, card, PayPal, and Venmo funding paths. Works in California and Texas where traditional sports betting is limited. Clean web and app experience under federal oversight.

**Where Polymarket wins:** Esports catalog breadth, depth on Tier 1 CS2 Majors, and map-level markets. Kalshi's CS2 book depth runs lower than Polymarket's on flagship match events. Taker fees are higher at mid-probability prices ($1.75 vs $0.75 on 100 shares at 50¢).

**On ClutchComet:** Tradeable. Linked on **39** esports matched rows vs Polymarket's **109**.

## Limitless: Base-native speed

Limitless runs on Coinbase's Base chain with USDC settlement and a central limit order book. Cumulative volume claims range from roughly **$270M to $497M** depending on source.

**What it does better than Polymarket:** Fast on-chain settlement on Base. Can lead on specific esports and crypto lines when its book is active. Maker fees are 0% on limit orders.

**Where Polymarket wins:** Esports catalog size and Tier 1 liquidity. Limitless links on **29** esports rows on ClutchComet vs Polymarket's **109**. CLOB buy fees at 50¢ ($1.50 on 100 shares) exceed Polymarket sports taker fees ($0.75).

**On ClutchComet:** Tradeable.

## Predict.fun: BNB ecosystem growth

Predict.fun operates on BNB Chain with taker-only fees. DefiLlama reports roughly **$2.22B** cumulative DEX volume and about **$280M** in 30-day volume as of the June 2026 snapshot.

**What it does better than Polymarket:** Accessible globally without Polymarket's main-platform geoblock complexity if you are already in the BNB ecosystem. Binance Wallet integration drove 2026 growth.

**Where Polymarket wins:** Volume, esports breadth, and institutional visibility. Predict.fun links on only **5** esports matched rows on ClutchComet. No U.S. consumer regulatory wrapper.

**On ClutchComet:** Tradeable.

## Forkast: the esports-native platform

The closest thing to a Polymarket alternative built specifically for gaming. Forkast migrated to **Arbitrum** in November 2025 and focuses on esports, gaming culture, and internet culture markets. Active esports weeks have shown roughly **$100K-$110K** weekly volume per industry reports.

**What it does better than Polymarket:** Esports-first UI and catalog orientation. Promo **zero fees through June 2026** on the standard Forkast schedule. Targets non-U.S. esports communities where Polymarket reach is inconsistent.

**Where Polymarket wins:** Liquidity by a wide margin. Forkast per-market volume is substantially lower than Polymarket on comparable Tier 1 CS2 matches. You trade specialization for depth.

**On ClutchComet:** Comparison-only. Useful as a price reference when the feed connects on a matched row.

## SX.bet: zero-fee singles on a sports exchange

SX.bet is a sports-native on-chain exchange on SX Rollup. DefiLlama reports roughly **$668.6M** cumulative DEX volume; SX blog cites about **$1.2B** cumulative sports volume under a broader methodology. **Not available in the United States** per SX Bet documentation.

**What it does better than Polymarket:** **0% maker and 0% taker on single bets.** Peer-to-peer exchange structure. Parlays carry **5% on profit only** (winning bets). Strong for non-U.S. sports and esports exchange traders who want singles without per-fill taker drag.

**Where Polymarket wins:** Esports title breadth, U.S. accessibility via Polymarket US, and Tier 1 CS2 depth. SX.bet is a global non-U.S. product.

**On ClutchComet:** Comparison-only (shown as **SX** in the UI).

## BetDEX: Solana peer-to-peer exchange

BetDEX runs on Solana via the Monaco protocol. New registrations from April 2026 use an **Anjouan** license; prior registrants remain under **Irish Revenue Commissioners** regulations. BetDEX publishes a **24-country prohibited list** in its terms.

**What it does better than Polymarket:** **1% commission on net profit only** when you win. No fee on entry or losing trades. Peer-to-peer sports exchange on Solana with KYC at certain thresholds.

**Where Polymarket wins:** Esports liquidity. BetDEX aggregate volume is not publicly reported. Major CS2 match books on Polymarket are much deeper. Not a U.S. consumer product.

**On ClutchComet:** Comparison-only.

## Myriad: low peak taker fees

Myriad Markets operates on BSC, Abstract, and Linea. DefiLlama reports roughly **$228.6M** cumulative DEX volume. Press coverage cites **400K+** active traders and **6.3M+** trades.

**What it does better than Polymarket:** Peak taker fee of **1.5%** at 50¢ (~$0.75 on 100 shares), matching Polymarket sports taker cost at the midpoint with 0% makers. Multi-chain deployment for crypto-native traders.

**Where Polymarket wins:** Esports catalog depth and Tier 1 liquidity. Myriad 30-day DEX volume (~$2.9M) is a fraction of Polymarket's monthly flow.

**On ClutchComet:** Comparison-only when WS feed links for a row.

**What it does better than Polymarket:** Unified account for perps and outcome markets under shared collateral. **No fee on open**; settlement tier-0 taker **0.045%** on $1/share. Attractive if you already trade Hyperliquid perps.

**Where Polymarket wins:** Everything esports-specific today. HIP-4 coverage is still early. ClutchComet shows HL outcome prices on matched rows when linked, not the full perp stack.

## The honest conclusion

**Better access.** Kalshi for U.S. traders without crypto wallet friction.

**Better fit for a trading style.** SX.bet and BetDEX for P2P exchange structures with profit-based fees. Limitless and Predict for crypto-native execution on integrated ClutchComet books. Myriad for low peak taker fees when linked.
