Myriad Markets reports 400K+ active traders in press coverage with a 1.5% peak taker fee on order-book markets. On ClutchComet, Myriad is comparison-only: you see Myriad prices on All Odds when the feed links a matched row, then execute on Polymarket, Kalshi, Limitless, or Predict from one balance.
