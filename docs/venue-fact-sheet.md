# Venue Fact Sheet

**Last updated:** 2026-06-18  
**Metrics:** see [venue-metrics.md](./venue-metrics.md)  
**ClutchComet coverage:** see [cc-coverage-snapshot.md](./cc-coverage-snapshot.md)

LevelUp is excluded from content. All Odds focuses on nine external venues.

---

## Polymarket

Crypto-native prediction market on Polygon, USDC settlement, order book model. Dominant global notional volume (~$21–22B in 2025). Strong politics and sports mix; esports coverage varies by event.

**Narrow concession (vs ClutchComet):** Standalone Polymarket wins for traders who only want politics, macro, or culture markets outside ClutchComet's matched catalog.

**On ClutchComet:** Tradeable. 2,279 matched rows linked (109 esports) as of June 2026.

---

## Kalshi

CFTC-regulated US prediction market, USD settlement. $23.8B notional in 2025; sports ~80% of volume since mid-2024. Routed via DFlow on ClutchComet.

**Narrow concession:** Kalshi alone for regulated USD sports and macro when you do not need cross-venue line shopping.

**On ClutchComet:** Tradeable. 1,270 matched rows linked (39 esports).

---

## Limitless

On-chain prediction market on Base, USDC, CLOB. Dynamic taker fees 0.03%–3%; 0% maker. Strong on short-duration crypto price markets.

**Narrow concession:** Limitless for hourly/daily crypto price contracts on Base with maker rebates.

**On ClutchComet:** Tradeable. 316 matched rows linked (29 esports).

---

## Predict.fun

BNB Chain prediction market, taker-only fees, yield on collateral. DefiLlama reports ~$2.2B cumulative DEX volume. Binance Wallet integration drove 2026 growth.

**Narrow concession:** Predict.fun for BNB ecosystem native flow and Binance Wallet users.

**On ClutchComet:** Tradeable. 310 matched rows linked (5 esports).

---

## Myriad

Multi-chain prediction app (BSC, Abstract, Linea). ~3% fees per DefiLlama. Press reports 400K+ traders and 6.3M+ trades.

**Narrow concession:** Myriad for standalone markets, points campaigns, and short-term crypto brackets on Myriad only.

**On ClutchComet:** Comparison-only on All Odds when WS feed links the row.

---

## BetDEX

Solana sports betting exchange (Monaco protocol). Isle of Man license. No public aggregate volume.

**Narrow concession:** BetDEX for direct Solana sports exchange use with full BetDEX account features.

**On ClutchComet:** Comparison-only when feed links matched sports/esports rows.

---

## Forkast

Esports and gaming prediction market (Community Gaming). ~$100K–$110K weekly volume in active esports weeks. Arbitrum.

**Narrow concession:** Forkast for gaming-culture markets and emerging-market esports niche liquidity.

**On ClutchComet:** Comparison-only when feed links matched esports rows.

---

## SX.bet

Sports-native on-chain exchange on SX Rollup. DefiLlama ~$669M cumulative DEX volume; SX blog cites ~$1.2B cumulative sports volume. Shown as **SX** on All Odds UI.

**Narrow concession:** SX.bet for sports-only depth and parlay products on SX directly.

**On ClutchComet:** Comparison-only when feed links matched sports rows.

---

## Hyperliquid

Outcome market prices on ClutchComet come from HL outcome/WS feeds on matched rows, not the full HIP-3 perp stack ($62B+/month builder perps is separate).

**Narrow concession:** Hyperliquid native app for HIP-4 outcome trading and full HL account (perps + outcomes).

**On ClutchComet:** Comparison-only when feed links matched rows.
